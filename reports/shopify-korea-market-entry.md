## Strategic Recommendation

### Verdict: **ENTER — but with a non-traditional SEO approach**

**Overall Score: 2.8/5 — Opportunity Market**

The Korean e-commerce platform market is fundamentally different from Western markets. Generic "build a store" keywords have almost no search volume because Koreans go directly to established local platforms. Shopify's current strategy of ranking for **informational content** (make money, dropshipping, business plans) is actually the right approach — it builds awareness before the purchase decision.

### Immediate Actions (30 days)
1. **Localize /tools/logo-maker to /kr/** — It's getting 1,311 visits from Korea without localization
2. **Localize /pricing to /kr/pricing** — Critical conversion page still in English (255 visits leaked)
3. **Fix English page leakage** — Add proper hreflang tags to prevent EN versions ranking in KR
4. **Redirect /store-login for Korean users** to a Korean-language login experience

### Short-term (30-90 days)
1. **Double down on dropshipping content** — "드랍쉬핑" keywords are low-competition and Shopify owns them. Create a Korean dropshipping content hub
2. **Target "부업" (side hustle) keywords** — "집에서 할수있는 부업" (home side hustles) has 2,500/mo volume at position 5. Optimize to reach top 3
3. **Create Korean case studies** — No Korean success story content exists. Local proof is critical in Korea
4. **Build comparison content** — "Shopify vs Cafe24", "Shopify vs Naver SmartStore" in Korean

### Long-term (90-180 days)
1. **Partner with Korean influencers/creators** — Brand search is the primary discovery channel in Korea, not generic keywords
2. **Target adjacent high-volume keywords** — "로고 디자인" (logo design, 4,200/mo), "사업계획서" (business plan, 3,200/mo), "메타 광고" (Meta ads, 2,900/mo) to build top-of-funnel
3. **Build Korean link profile** — Get links from i-boss.co.kr community, Korean tech blogs, and Korean startup media
4. **Consider Korean social SEO** — Naver Blog and Naver Cafe are major discovery channels in Korea that traditional SEO misses

### Estimated Investment: **Medium**
### Expected Timeline to ROI: **9-12 months**

The Korean market won't deliver massive organic traffic from generic keywords — it's not how Koreans discover e-commerce tools. Instead, focus on branded search growth, informational content that builds trust, and partnerships that drive direct navigation. The $2.85M traffic value on just 46K visits shows Korean traffic is **extremely valuable** ($61.96/visit) — even small gains in traffic deliver outsized returns.
