# Localization Gap Analysis: ahrefs.com

**Date:** March 2025
**Domain:** ahrefs.com (DR 91 | Ahrefs Rank #822)
**Markets Analyzed:** US (baseline), Germany, France, Spain, Brazil, Japan

---

## Executive Summary

Ahrefs has a well-structured localization setup with `/de/`, `/fr/`, `/es/`, `/pt/`, `/ja/` locale paths and localized blog content at `/blog/de/`, `/blog/fr/`, `/blog/es/`. However, **massive English page leakage exists across every market** — particularly for SEO tool pages (`/keyword-rank-checker`, `/backlink-checker`, `/traffic-checker`) which have NO localized versions and are pulling tens of thousands of visits from non-English countries. The writing tools are much better localized, but several high-traffic English writing tools still leak. **Total estimated leaked traffic from just the top gaps: ~130K+ visits/month across 5 markets.**
